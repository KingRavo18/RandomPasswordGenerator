This is a little Random Password Generator project, made using html, css and typescript. 
When you press the generate button, a 15 character long password will be generated, 
encompassing at least 1 number,
at least 1 uncapitalized letter, 
at least 1 capitalized letter, 
and at least 1 special symbol.
By pressing the copy button, you can then copy the password to you clipboard.
